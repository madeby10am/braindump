BrainDump

Talk messy. Paste clean.

Hold a key and ramble. BrainDump transcribes with Whisper, tidies it with a small AI model, and pastes clean text, a structured Claude prompt, an email, or a Slack message at your cursor. Everything runs on your Mac. No cloud, no accounts, no API keys.

Install on Mac
git clone https://github.com/madeby10am/braindump.git ~/BrainDump && cd ~/BrainDump && brew install whisper-cpp llama.cpp && bash scripts/install-braindump.sh

How it works

Ramble in. Clean text out.

01
Hold
Press and hold your hotkey (Option by default). Recording starts instantly. Pick Hold, Toggle or Auto-stop mode.
02
Ramble
Say it however it comes out. Whisper transcribes on your Mac, then a local model removes the ums, fixes the structure and applies your chosen style.
03
Paste
Clean text lands at your cursor in any app. Short dictations skip the model and paste instantly.

Install flow

Five steps. A few minutes.

01
Paste the install command. It needs an Apple Silicon Mac with Homebrew and Apple's command-line tools (xcode-select --install). It builds BrainDump and installs it to ~/Applications.
02
Grant Microphone permission. A system dialog appears. Click Allow. One time only.
03
Grant Accessibility permission. System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. Add BrainDump and switch it on.
04
Models download on first use. About 3 GB total for the speech and formatting models, stored in ~/.config/braindump/models.
05
Start talking. BrainDump lives in your menu bar and opens at login. Hold Option, speak, release.

Features

Built for people who think out loud.

Private

Speech and formatting both run on your Mac. No cloud, no accounts, no telemetry. Dictation history stays in a local file.

Five styles

Clean Up, Claude Prompt, Email, Slack, or Custom with your own instructions. Switch from the menu bar or settings.

Fast

Whisper uses Apple Silicon. Dictations under 15 words skip the formatter and paste instantly. If the model is slow or down, you get the raw transcript.

Your music keeps playing

BrainDump does not lower other audio while you dictate. Opt in from the settings gear menu if you want noise reduction instead.

Settings you can see

A settings window with a live Try-it box, a History tab of your last 50 dictations, a clickable hotkey picker, and light or dark mode.

Works everywhere

Text appears at your cursor in any app: editors, browsers, terminals, Slack, email.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is BrainDump free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT license. No subscription, no API keys.

Does any audio or text leave my computer?

No. Whisper transcribes on your Mac and the formatting model runs locally through llama.cpp. Your last 50 dictations are kept in a local history file that you can clear.

Will it lower the volume of my music while I dictate?

No. Other audio keeps playing at full volume by default. Turn on “Lower other audio while recording” in the settings gear menu if you prefer noise reduction.

What Macs are supported?

Apple Silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 13 or newer. Intel Macs are not supported. Plan for about 3 GB of disk for the models.

macOS says it can’t verify the app. What do I do?

The install script builds BrainDump on your own Mac, so this normally does not appear. If you copy the app from another Mac, right-click it in Applications and choose Open, or allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Where does this come from?

BrainDump is a fork of open-wispr by Ammon Taylor (MIT) that adds local AI formatting, styles, a settings window and history.
